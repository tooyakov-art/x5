This build completes the requested course, notification, chat, Home, purchase
and portfolio fixes in Xfive marketing.

- The app name is now written consistently as “Xfive marketing” everywhere.
- Full-length lesson videos upload without truncation. Oversized sources are
  prepared locally and continue through resumable private upload.
- Task notifications open the exact task; message notifications open the exact
  chat even after a cold launch.
- Chat photo, video and voice media use private storage and short-lived links.
- Portfolio photos and video previews use private storage and are checked
  automatically before publication. There is no manual-review queue.
- CourseUP management remains restricted to the two authorized developer
  accounts, with corrected typography and retry-safe uploads.

- One-time 1,000/2,000/5,000 credit packs stay consumable and can be purchased
  again. Interrupted delivery retries safely after relaunch.
- Digital credit packs now use one direct StoreKit purchase button. Alternate
  payment choices and external checkout routes were removed from the iOS app.
- The Verified Badge action keeps readable black text on its bright button.
- Muted Home card previews pause offscreen, while the app is inactive or when
  Reduce Motion is enabled; static posters remain as a safe fallback.
- AI Voice now uses a protected server-side MiniMax route to turn text into
  playable and shareable MP3 audio.
- AI Video is live through the official BytePlus Seedance 2.0 Fast route. A
  completed private MP4 can be played, shared or saved to Photos.
- AI Studio now shows only tools whose provider passed the authenticated live
  health check. Placeholder and "In Development" cards are no longer shown.
- Working MiniMax voice, Seedance video, Cinema, VFX, Presets and the private
  cloud gallery open directly and return real results.
- Rejected provider requests are not charged, or are refunded automatically.
- Home cards now share one clear typography system. Every Hub profession has
  its own icon, and SMM with mobile content is one category everywhere.
