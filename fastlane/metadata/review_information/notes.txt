Xfive marketing is a creator workspace and marketplace for creators, marketers,
specialists and clients. Version 1.1.4 build 180 includes Home creator tools,
image generation, Generated Gallery, Hub, Chats, Portfolio, CourseUP, Profile,
one-time credit packs, verified badge, report/block controls and account deletion.

Demo account:
Use the credentials in the Apple Demo Account fields:
appreview@x5studio.app

The account has a profile, sample portfolio content, chats and credits for
testing image generation. Sign in with Apple or Google may also be used.

Quick test flow:
1. Sign in with the demo email and password, Apple or Google.
2. Home tab - open Image generation, Logo, Story, Post, Product photo or
   Packaging.
3. Image generator - enter a prompt, optionally attach reference images, choose
   model/size/quantity and tap Generate.
4. Generated result - preview, save to in-app gallery, save to Photos, share,
   markup or regenerate from edits.
5. Hub - switch between Specialists and Tasks, open a specialist/task, start a
   chat or send a proposal.
6. Chats - send text, attach a photo, record voice, search, reply, pin, mute,
   archive, report or block.
7. Portfolio/Profile - add photos or videos, edit public visibility and social
   links.
8. CourseUP - open a course, preview a lesson and buy a paid course with credits.
9. Profile -> Store - review and purchase a one-time 1,000, 2,000 or 5,000
   credit pack. Profile -> Get verified opens the separate monthly verified
   badge subscription with restore and management controls.
10. Profile -> Settings - language, Face ID lock, cache, notifications, sign
    out and Delete Account.

Image generation:
The app sends the user's prompt and optional reference images to the Xfive marketing Supabase
Edge Function generate-image. Generation is credit-based and the UI shows cost
before generation. Generated images can be kept on-device, saved to Photos or
shared. Reference images are chosen by the user through the iOS photo picker.
Unfinished video/startup-chat cards are marked as in development.

Credit purchases and verification:
1,000 Credits: com.x5studio.app.credits.1000 (consumable).
2,000 Credits: com.x5studio.app.credits.2000 (consumable).
5,000 Credits: com.x5studio.app.credits.5000 (consumable).
Verified Badge Monthly: com.x5studio.app.verified.monthly (auto-renewable).
The Store is available from every Profile and shows the current server balance.
A credit purchase changes only that balance; it does not activate Pro or change
subscription dates. Consumables are not shown as restorable. The verified badge
is a separate 1,000 KZT/month subscription from Profile -> Get verified.

Existing Lite, Pro and Max subscriptions remain recognized and restorable for
current subscribers, but they are not offered in the new Store:
com.x5studio.app.lite.monthly, com.x5studio.app.pro.monthly and
com.x5studio.app.max.monthly.

UGC safety:
Every chat user can be blocked or reported from the chat header menu. Hub users
and task authors can be reported. Reports open an email to support and are
reviewed within 24 hours. Users can delete their account from Settings; the app
calls Supabase delete_own_account and signs the user out.

Biometrics:
The app does not collect, store, transmit or process face data. Apple's
LocalAuthentication framework is used only for optional App Lock. Xfive marketing receives
only success/fail result and no biometric data.

Permissions:
Photos are used for avatars, portfolio, chat attachments, reference images and
saving generated images. Microphone is used only for chat voice messages. APNs
is used for message, proposal and optional promo alerts.
