Xfive marketing is a creator workspace and marketplace. Version 1.1.6 build 188
includes creator tools, image generation, Hub, Chats, Portfolio, CourseUP,
Profile, one-time credit packs, verified badge, UGC controls and account
deletion.

Demo account:
Use the Apple Demo Account credentials for appreview@x5studio.app. The account
has sample content, chats and credits. Apple, Google and email sign-in are
supported.

Quick test:
1. Sign in.
2. Home -> Image generation: enter a prompt, optionally add references, choose
   size/quantity and generate. Results can be previewed, saved and shared.
3. Hub -> Tasks: tap the category filter next to All, select several categories,
   open a task, then use Back to categories.
4. Chats: send text/photo/voice; reply, search, pin, mute, archive, report or
   block.
5. Portfolio/Profile: add a photo or video from the photo library. Safe images
   are checked automatically; videos and inconclusive items enter the
   developer-only review queue.
6. CourseUP: open a course, tap the author to open that exact public profile,
   preview a lesson and buy a paid course with credits. Developer accounts can
   select a real author and manage lesson videos with visible upload progress.
7. Profile -> Store: buy one-time 1,000/2,000/5,000-credit packs. Profile ->
   Get verified opens the separate monthly verified-badge subscription.
8. Settings: language, Face ID lock, notifications, sign out and Delete Account.

Data processing and moderation:
- Image generation sends the user's prompt and optional selected reference
  images to the Xfive Supabase Edge Function generate-image.
- For portfolio safety, portfolio titles/descriptions and user-selected images
  are sent by our Supabase Edge Function to OpenAI's moderation API. Videos are
  not sent to OpenAI; they require developer review.
- The app does not use third-party advertising trackers.

In-app purchases:
- com.x5studio.app.credits.1000 / .2000 / .5000 are consumables.
- com.x5studio.app.verified.monthly is an auto-renewable subscription.
Credit packs change only the signed-in server balance and are applied exactly
once from Apple's signed transaction. Unfinished delivery is retried. The
verified badge is separate and does not include credits.

Existing Lite/Pro/Max subscriptions remain recognized for current subscribers
but are not offered in the new Store.

UGC safety:
Chat users, Hub users and task authors can be reported; chat users can be
blocked. Reports go to support and are reviewed within 24 hours. Account
deletion is available in Settings.

Biometrics:
LocalAuthentication is used only for optional App Lock. No face or biometric
data is collected, stored or transmitted.

Permissions:
Photos: avatars, portfolio, chats, references and saving results.
Microphone: chat voice messages. APNs: message/proposal/optional promo alerts.
