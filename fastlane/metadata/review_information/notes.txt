X five marketing is a creator workspace and marketplace. Version 1.1.6 build 234
fixes the App Review completeness issue from the previous binary.

This build has no placeholder, fake-progress or "In Development" AI cards.
Home, Search and AI Studio fetch the authenticated server capability catalog
and display only tools whose provider is configured and has passed its live
health check. A provider outage cannot charge the user.

Demo account:
Use the Apple Demo Account credentials for appreview@x5studio.app.

Quick test:
1. Sign in, then open Home -> All AI Tools. Every visible generation card is
   enabled and opens a working flow.
2. AI Voice: enter a short Russian, Kazakh or English text, choose a MiniMax
   voice, generate, play and share the resulting private MP3.
3. AI Video: enter a safe prompt, keep Seedance 2.0 Fast, select 5 seconds and
   720p, then generate. The private MP4 can be played, shared or saved to Photos.
4. Cinema Studio uses scene, style, camera and light inputs. VFX animates an
   uploaded photo. Both use the same verified Seedance route.
5. Live Products: complete the fields, create/edit the three-scene storyboard,
   then generate its reference and video.
6. Startup Chat: ask a business question.
7. Hub: browse specialists and tasks by country, city and profession.
8. CourseUP: open a course, author profile and lesson. Paid courses can be
   purchased. Only two authorized accounts can manage courses.
9. Portfolio/Profile: add a photo or video. Automatic moderation keeps pending
   or rejected media private.
10. Chats: send text/photo/voice; report or block another user.
11. Profile -> Store: buy a one-time 1,000/2,000/5,000-credit pack. Get
   verified opens a separate monthly subscription.
12. Settings: change language or delete the account.

AI processing and safety:
- MiniMax Speech creates requested voiceovers and stores MP3 results privately.
- BytePlus ModelArk Seedance creates requested videos; results are copied to
  private storage and delivered with short-lived signed URLs.
- OpenAI powers Startup Chat, Live Products storyboards and automatic safety
  moderation. Unsafe requests fail before credits are reserved.
- The app does not use third-party advertising trackers.

In-app purchases:
- com.x5studio.app.credits.1000 / .2000 / .5000 are consumables.
- com.x5studio.app.verified.monthly is an auto-renewable subscription.
Credit packs are delivered exactly once from Apple's signed transaction. The
verified badge is separate and does not include credits.

UGC safety:
Chat users, Hub users and task authors can be reported; chat users can be
blocked. Account deletion is available in Settings.

Permissions:
Photos: course media, avatars, portfolio, chats, AI references and saving
generated media. Microphone: chat voice messages. APNs: notifications.
