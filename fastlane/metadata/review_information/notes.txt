X5 Creators is a marketplace and messaging app for creators and clients in
Kazakhstan and Central Asia. The app includes Hub, Chats, Portfolio, Courses
and Profile.

REVIEWER ANSWERS TO PREVIOUS REJECTION

(1) Face Data - Guideline 2.1
The app does not collect, store, transmit or process face data. Apple's
LocalAuthentication framework is used only for optional App Lock in Settings.
Apple handles biometric matching on device; X5 receives only a Boolean
success/fail result and no biometric data. The privacy policy explains this
under "Why we ask for permissions":
https://tooyakov-art.github.io/x5site/privacy.html

(2) Objectionable Content - Guideline 1.1.1
The previous AI-face and battle-style promotional screens are not part of this
submission. Store screenshots now show only review-safe marketplace, chat,
portfolio, course and profile flows. The app does not include content that
targets race, religion, gender, violence or protected groups.

(3) IAP not submitted - Guideline 2.1(b)
X5 Pro Monthly is the only paid subscription product used by this submission.
The paywall is available from Profile -> Upgrade to Pro. A paywall review
screenshot has been uploaded to the subscription product.

(4) Home tab "fetched no contents" on iPad - Guideline 2.1(a)
Fixed. The previous gate that hid content for non-developer accounts is
removed; live tools and app sections display directly to all users.

DEMO ACCOUNT
Use the credentials in the Apple Demo Account fields. The account has a small
portfolio, free credits and a public profile already set up. There is one
existing chat with sample messages.

QUICK TEST FLOW
1. Sign in with Apple, Google, or the demo email + password.
2. Hub tab - browse tasks, tap one, send a proposal.
3. Chats tab - open the demo conversation; send text, attach a photo
   (paperclip -> Photos), record a voice message (long-press mic). Top-right
   menu -> Open profile, Report, Block. Swipe a row -> Pin, Mute or Delete.
4. Portfolio - open Profile -> Portfolio and tap "+" to add a portfolio item.
5. Courses tab - open the free course and play any lesson.
6. Profile -> Settings - Face ID lock, notifications, cache, sign out and
   Delete Account.
7. Profile -> Upgrade to Pro - verify subscription terms, Restore Purchases
   and Manage Subscription controls.

UGC SAFETY - Guideline 1.2
- Every chat user can be blocked or reported from the chat header menu.
- Hub task authors can be blocked from profile or reported.
- Reports open an email to the moderation team and are reviewed within
  24 hours.
- Account deletion in Settings is two-step and removes auth, profile,
  portfolio, tasks and messages permanently.

SUBSCRIPTION - Guideline 3.1.2
X5 Pro is an optional auto-renewing monthly subscription at $9.99/month. The
paywall shows subscription length, price, auto-renewal terms, Apple's Standard
EULA link, a Privacy Policy link, Restore Purchases and Manage Subscription.

PUSH NOTIFICATIONS
APNs is used to deliver new-message and accepted-proposal alerts. Permission is
requested only after the user signs in.

PRIVACY
Sign in with Apple is offered alongside Google and email. X5 does not use
tracking, ads or third-party advertising SDKs. The privacy policy accurately
lists photos, microphone, notifications, profile, chat and portfolio data:
https://tooyakov-art.github.io/x5site/privacy.html

If anything is unclear: support@x5studio.app.
