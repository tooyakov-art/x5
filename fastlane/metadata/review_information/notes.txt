Xfive marketing. Version 1.1.9 build 239.
This update validates the selected StoreKit product before reporting purchase
success and clarifies credit confirmation messages. It retains the reviewed
1.1.8 feature set; no Kaspi, external checkout, or paid-lesson feature is added.

Profile -> Store offers repeatable consumable packs of 1000, 2000 and 5000
credits. Verified badge is a separate auto-renewable monthly subscription.
Prices are loaded from StoreKit. Cancelled, pending or unverified purchases
do not show success. Server verification and exact-once delivery remain in place.

Use the supplied appreview@x5studio.app demo credentials. The review account
has a capped isolated Sandbox allowance to exercise the purchase flow.
Ordinary TestFlight accounts cannot receive spendable credits from Sandbox.
Production purchases require Apple's signed transaction verification.

Mainland China remains excluded from availability. Existing privacy disclosures,
AI capabilities, account deletion and portfolio moderation are unchanged.
