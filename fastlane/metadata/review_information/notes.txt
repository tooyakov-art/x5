X five marketing is a creator workspace and marketplace. Version 1.1.6 build 232
adds AI Video plus course, notification, chat, purchase, Home and portfolio fixes.

Demo account:
Use the Apple Demo Account credentials for appreview@x5studio.app.

Quick test:
1. Sign in.
2. Home -> AI Video: enter a safe text prompt, keep Seedance 2.0 Fast, select
   5 seconds and 720p, then generate. The demo account includes review credits.
   The completed private MP4 can be played, shared or saved to Photos.
3. Home -> AI Image or AI Influencer: each unfinished tool shows a clear In
   Development screen and does not charge credits or call a provider.
4. Home -> AI Voice: enter a short text, choose a voice, generate it, then play
   or share the resulting MP3. The request uses the authenticated server route.
5. Home -> Startup Chat: ask a business question.
6. Home -> Live Fruits: complete the fields, create/edit the three-scene
   storyboard, then generate its reference and video.
7. Hub: every profession has a distinct system icon. SMM and mobile content are
   one category. The same catalog is used in onboarding, profile and tasks.
8. CourseUP: open a course, its author profile and a lesson; paid courses can be
   purchased. Only two authorized accounts can manage courses.
9. Portfolio/Profile: add a photo or video. Photos and video previews are
   checked automatically; pending or rejected items never become public.
10. Chats: send text/photo/voice; report or block another user.
11. Profile -> Store: buy one-time 1,000/2,000/5,000-credit packs. Each is an
    Apple consumable and remains repurchasable. Get verified opens a separate
    monthly subscription; its bright action button now uses black text.
12. Settings: change language or delete the account.

AI processing and automatic moderation:
- AI Image and AI Influencer remain release-gated and do not call providers or
  use credits in this build.
- AI Video is live through the authenticated official BytePlus ModelArk
  Seedance 2.0 Fast route. It costs 650 credits for 5 seconds or 1,200 credits
  for 10 seconds. OpenAI is the primary input safety check; Google Gemini and
  BytePlus ModelArk are failover safety classifiers. If all checks are
  unavailable, the request fails before any credit reservation. Results are
  copied to private storage and downloaded through a short-lived signed URL
  before local playback, sharing or Photos saving.
- AI Voice is live through an authenticated Supabase Edge Function. It uses
  MiniMax Speech, stores the MP3 privately, returns a short-lived signed URL and
  charges 60 credits per started 1,000-character block.
- Startup Chat uses OpenAI Responses with storage disabled. Live Fruits uses
  OpenAI Moderations and Responses.
- Portfolio moderation is automatic through OpenAI Moderations; pending or
  failed checks stay private.
- The app does not use third-party advertising trackers.

In-app purchases:
- com.x5studio.app.credits.1000 / .2000 / .5000 are consumables.
- com.x5studio.app.verified.monthly is an auto-renewable subscription.
Credit packs are delivered exactly once from Apple's signed transaction. The
verified badge is separate and does not include credits.

UGC safety:
Chat users, Hub users and task authors can be reported; chat users can be
blocked. Account deletion is available in Settings.

Permissions:
Photos: course media, avatars, portfolio, chats, AI references and saving
generated media. Microphone: chat voice messages. APNs: notifications.
