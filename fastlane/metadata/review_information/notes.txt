X five marketing is a creator workspace and marketplace. Version 1.1.6 build 191
adds the new AI home, Startup Chat, AI Video, the Live Fruits storyboard,
course-upload reliability improvements, Hub category filtering and purchase
delivery fixes.

Demo account:
Use the Apple Demo Account credentials for appreview@x5studio.app. The account
has sample content, chats and credits.

Quick test:
1. Sign in.
2. Home -> AI Image: enter a prompt, optionally add references, choose
   size/quantity and generate. Results can be previewed, saved and shared.
3. Home -> Startup Chat: ask a business question.
4. Home -> AI Video: enter a prompt, optionally select a photo, choose 5 or 10
   seconds and submit. Progress remains in Recent generations after relaunch.
5. Home -> Live Fruits: complete the six short fields, generate the exact
   three-scene storyboard, edit/reorder scenes, generate a character reference
   and frames, then create the 9:16 video.
6. Hub -> Tasks: tap "Tasks for my categories" next to All. Categories saved in
   the signed-in profile are applied immediately.
7. Chats: send text/photo/voice; reply, search, pin, mute, archive, report or
   block.
8. Portfolio/Profile: add a photo or video from the photo library. Automatic
   safety checks prevent prohibited media from becoming public.
9. CourseUP: open a course, tap the author to open that exact public profile,
   preview a lesson and buy a paid course. The two authorized developer
   accounts can select authors and manage lesson videos with resumable uploads.
10. Profile -> Store: buy one-time 1,000/2,000/5,000-credit packs. Each item is
    an Apple consumable and remains repurchasable. Get verified opens the
    separate monthly verified-badge subscription.
11. Settings: language, Face ID lock, notifications, sign out and Delete
    Account.

AI processing and automatic moderation:
- AI Image sends the prompt and optional selected reference images through the
  Supabase Edge Function generate-image to the selected image provider.
- Startup Chat sends the displayed conversation to OpenAI Responses with
  server-side authentication and storage disabled.
- Live Fruits sends the questionnaire to OpenAI Moderations and Responses to
  produce the structured three-scene storyboard. Generated frames reuse the
  existing image-generation service.
- AI Video sends the prompt and optional selected reference image first to
  OpenAI Moderations. A flagged request is rejected before credits are
  reserved. Accepted requests are processed asynchronously by fal Kling Video,
  Google Gemini Omni Flash, or OpenAI Sora, depending on configured provider
  availability. Inputs/results are private and the app receives only
  short-lived signed result URLs.
- The app does not use third-party advertising trackers.

In-app purchases:
- com.x5studio.app.credits.1000 / .2000 / .5000 are consumables.
- com.x5studio.app.verified.monthly is an auto-renewable subscription.
Credit packs are delivered exactly once from Apple's signed transaction.
Unfinished delivery survives relaunch and is retried.
The verified badge is separate and does not include credits.

Existing Lite/Pro/Max subscriptions remain recognized for current subscribers
but are not offered in the new Store.

UGC safety:
Chat users, Hub users and task authors can be reported; chat users can be
blocked. Account deletion is available in Settings.

Permissions:
Photos: course videos/covers, avatars, portfolio, chats, AI references and
saving generated images/videos.
Microphone: chat voice messages. APNs: message/proposal/optional promo alerts.
