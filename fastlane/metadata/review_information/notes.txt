X5 Creators is a marketplace and messaging app for creators (photographers,
designers, video editors, copywriters, SMM managers) and clients in
Kazakhstan and Central Asia. The app has four main areas: Hub (open tasks
from clients), Chats, Portfolio and Courses.

REVIEWER ANSWERS TO PREVIOUS REJECTION

(1) Face Data — Guideline 2.1
The app does not collect, store, transmit or process face data. Apple's
LocalAuthentication framework is used only for an optional App Lock that
the user can enable in Settings. Apple's Secure Enclave handles biometric
matching on device; the app receives only a Boolean success/fail result
and no biometric data. See privacy policy section "Why we ask for
permissions → Face ID":
https://tooyakov-art.github.io/x5site/privacy.html

(2) Objectionable Content — Guideline 1.1.1
The Home banners and tool grid that previously contained AI-generated
faces and a battle scene have been removed in this build. The Home tab
now shows only working tools (Captions, Academy). No imagery referencing
race, religion, gender, violence or related themes remains in the app
or in the App Store screenshots.

(3) IAP not submitted — Guideline 2.1(b)
X5 Pro Monthly subscription is included as an item in this submission.
A review screenshot of the paywall has been uploaded to the subscription
product.

(4) Home tab "fetched no contents" on iPad — Guideline 2.1(a)
Fixed. The previous gate that hid the tool grid for non-developer
accounts is removed; live tools now display directly to all users.

DEMO ACCOUNT
Use the credentials in the Apple Demo Account fields. The account has a
small portfolio (3 sample items), 100 free credits, and a public profile
already set up. There is one existing chat with sample messages; you can
also open another account on a second device to test sending messages.

QUICK TEST FLOW
1. Sign in with Apple, Google, or the demo email + password.
2. Home tab — Captions opens the live caption template tool; Academy
   deep-links to the Courses tab.
3. Hub tab — browse tasks, tap one, send a proposal.
4. Chats tab — open the existing demo conversation; send text, attach a
   photo (paperclip → Photos), record a voice message (long-press mic).
   Top-right menu → Open profile, Report, Block. Swipe a row → Pin/Mute,
   or Delete.
5. Portfolio tab (Profile → Portfolio) — tap "+" to add a portfolio entry.
6. Courses tab — "AI для бизнеса" is free; tap to open and play any lesson.
7. Profile → Settings (gear icon) → Face ID lock, Promo notifications,
   Cache, Sign out, Delete account.

UGC SAFETY (Guideline 1.2)
- Every chat user can be Blocked or Reported from the chat header menu.
- Hub task authors can be Blocked from their profile or Reported.
- Reports open an email to our moderation team and are reviewed within
  24 hours.
- Account deletion in Settings is two-step and removes auth, profile,
  portfolio, tasks and messages permanently.

SUBSCRIPTION (Guideline 3.1.2)
X5 Pro is an auto-renewing monthly subscription at $9.99/month. The
paywall (Profile → Upgrade to Pro) shows: subscription length, price,
auto-renewal terms, Apple's Standard EULA link, and a Privacy Policy
link. Restore Purchases and Manage Subscription buttons are present.

PUSH NOTIFICATIONS
APNs is used to deliver new-message and accepted-proposal alerts. Permission
is requested only after the user signs in.

PRIVACY
Sign in with Apple is offered alongside Google and email (Guideline 4.8).
No third-party trackers, no ads, no analytics SDKs. The app does not use
App Tracking Transparency.

If anything is unclear: support@x5studio.app.
