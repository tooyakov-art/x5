X5 Creators is a creator workspace and marketplace app for creators,
marketers, specialists and clients. Build 143 includes image generation,
Generated Gallery, Hub, Chats, Portfolio, CourseUP, Profile, subscriptions,
credits, report/block controls and account deletion.

CURRENT APP VERSION

Version: 1.1.1
Build: 143

DEMO ACCOUNT

Use the credentials in the Apple Demo Account fields:
appreview@x5studio.app

The account has a profile, sample portfolio content, chats and credits for
testing image generation. Sign in with Apple or Google may also be used.

QUICK TEST FLOW

1. Sign in with the demo email and password, Apple or Google.
2. Home tab - tap "Image generation", "Logo", "Story", "Post", "Insta Pack",
   "Product photo" or "Packaging".
3. Image generator - enter a prompt, optionally attach reference images,
   choose model/size/quantity and tap Generate.
4. Generated result - preview the image, save it to the in-app Generated
   Gallery, save to Photos, share it, draw/markup it or regenerate from edits.
5. Generated Gallery - open the photo-stack button from Home or the generator,
   preview previous generated images, edit, share or delete them.
6. Hub tab - switch between Specialists and Tasks, open a specialist/task,
   start a chat or send a proposal.
7. Chats tab - open a conversation, send text, attach a photo, record a voice
   message, search, reply, pin, mute, archive, report or block.
8. Portfolio/Profile - add photos or videos, edit profile, public visibility
   and social links.
9. CourseUP tab - open a course and play a lesson. Pro-gated courses show the
   paywall.
10. Profile -> Upgrade to Pro - review subscription terms, purchase, restore
    and manage subscription controls.
11. Profile -> Settings - test language, Face ID app lock, cache, notifications,
    sign out and Delete Account.

IMAGE GENERATION

- The app sends the user's prompt and optional reference images to the X5
  Supabase Edge Function `generate-image`.
- Generation is credit-based. The UI shows the credit cost before generation.
- Generated images are returned to the app, saved in an on-device generated
  gallery and can optionally be saved to the user's Photos library.
- Reference images are chosen by the user through the iOS photo picker.
- Video generation and startup-chat tool cards that are not ready are marked
  in the app as in development / coming soon and are not sold as active
  functionality.

SUBSCRIPTION AND CREDITS

- X5 Pro Monthly: com.x5studio.app.pro.monthly
- The paywall is available from Profile -> Upgrade to Pro.
- X5 Pro grants Pro access and credits for creator tools.
- The paywall displays subscription length, price, auto-renewal terms, Apple's
  Standard EULA link, Privacy Policy, Restore Purchases and Manage Subscription.
- The verified badge is activated with in-app credits and is not a separate IAP
  in this build.

USER-GENERATED CONTENT AND SAFETY - GUIDELINE 1.2

- Every chat user can be blocked or reported from the chat header menu.
- Hub users and task authors can be contacted through chat and reported.
- Reports open an email to the moderation team and are reviewed within
  24 hours.
- Account deletion in Settings is two-step and removes auth, profile,
  portfolio, tasks and messages permanently.

FACE ID / BIOMETRICS

The app does not collect, store, transmit or process face data. Apple's
LocalAuthentication framework is used only for optional App Lock in Settings.
Apple handles biometric matching on device; X5 receives only a Boolean
success/fail result and no biometric data.

PHOTOS, MICROPHONE AND NOTIFICATIONS

- Photos access is used for avatars, portfolio uploads, chat attachments,
  reference images for image generation and saving generated images.
- Microphone access is used only for chat voice messages.
- APNs is used for new-message, accepted-proposal and optional promo alerts.
  Notification permission is requested only after the user signs in.

ACCOUNT DELETION - GUIDELINE 5.1.1(v)

1. Profile tab -> top-right gear icon -> Settings.
2. Scroll to Danger zone.
3. Tap Delete Account and confirm twice.
4. The app calls the Supabase `delete_own_account` RPC and signs the user out.
