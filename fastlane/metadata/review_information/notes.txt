X five marketing is a creator workspace and marketplace. Version 1.1.6 build 227
adds reliable full-length course uploads, exact task/chat notification routing,
private chat and portfolio media, safer automatic portfolio moderation,
AI Voice, repeatable credit packs, and the updated Home experience.

Demo account:
Use the Apple Demo Account credentials for appreview@x5studio.app.

Quick test:
1. Sign in.
2. Home -> AI Image: enter a prompt, optionally add references, choose
   size/quantity and generate.
3. Home -> AI Video or AI Influencer: both unfinished tools show a clear
   In Development screen and do not charge credits or call a provider.
4. Home -> AI Voice: enter text, choose voice/language/speed, generate, preview
   and share the audio.
5. Home -> Startup Chat: ask a business question.
6. Home -> Live Fruits: complete the fields, create/edit the three-scene
   storyboard, then generate its reference and video.
7. Hub: every profession has a distinct system icon. SMM and mobile content are
   one category. The same catalog is used in onboarding, profile and tasks.
8. CourseUP: open a course, tap the author to open that exact public profile,
   preview a lesson and buy a paid course. The two authorized developer
   accounts can select authors and manage lessons.
9. Portfolio/Profile: add a photo or video. Photos and video previews are
   checked automatically; pending or rejected items never become public.
10. Chats: send text/photo/voice; report or block another user.
11. Profile -> Store: buy one-time 1,000/2,000/5,000-credit packs. Each is an
    Apple consumable and remains repurchasable. Get verified opens a separate
    monthly subscription; its bright action button now uses black text.
12. Settings: change language or delete the account.

Home motion:
Muted card previews pause offscreen, in the background or with Reduce Motion.
A static poster remains if motion cannot play.

AI processing and automatic moderation:
- AI Image uses the authenticated generate-image Supabase Edge Function.
- AI Video and AI Influencer are release-gated until their complete generation
  flows pass production verification. They do not call providers or use credits.
- AI Voice sends entered text through the authenticated generate-voice Edge
  Function to the ElevenLabs Eleven v3 model via fal. Result audio is stored
  privately and returned through a short-lived signed URL.
- Startup Chat uses OpenAI Responses with storage disabled. Live Fruits uses
  OpenAI Moderations and Responses.
- Portfolio moderation is automatic-only through OpenAI Moderations. There is
  no developer/manual review queue. Provider failures stay private and retry.
- The app does not use third-party advertising trackers.

In-app purchases:
- com.x5studio.app.credits.1000 / .2000 / .5000 are consumables.
- com.x5studio.app.verified.monthly is an auto-renewable subscription.
Credit packs are delivered exactly once from Apple's signed transaction.
Unfinished delivery survives relaunch and retries without double crediting.
The verified badge is separate and does not include credits.

UGC safety:
Chat users, Hub users and task authors can be reported; chat users can be
blocked. Account deletion is available in Settings.

Permissions:
Photos: course media, avatars, portfolio, chats, AI references and saving
generated media. Microphone: chat voice messages. APNs: notifications.
